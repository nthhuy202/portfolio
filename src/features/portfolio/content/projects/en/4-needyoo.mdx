## Problem

Job seekers had to judge a role from a plain text listing, and recruiters had no fast, in-app way to reach candidates once they'd matched.

## Responsibilities

Built the core UI from Figma with role-based components so recruiters and job seekers see different views after login. Developed job and candidate lists with search and pagination, a TikTok-style video-reels page for job discovery, and real-time recruiter-candidate chat over Socket.IO. Configured Firebase push notifications to keep both sides updated on new activity.

## Result

Shipped across web and mobile as part of a 6-person team. The video-reels format gave candidates a faster way to evaluate a role before applying, and the chat plus push-notification loop kept conversations moving without leaving the app.
