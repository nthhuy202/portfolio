## Problem

Needyoo is a transparent recruitment ecosystem connecting candidates and employers through short-form video content (TikTok/Reels style). The critical business challenge was delivering a seamless, zero-latency video scrolling experience while strictly controlling massive AWS S3 storage and bandwidth costs generated by heavy media content.

## Responsibilities

Acted as the Lead Frontend Developer, managing a sub-team of 3 FEs within a 9-person agile team. Owned the frontend architecture, sprint estimations, task delegation, cross-code review processes, and the engineering of core video streaming logic.

## Challenges & Solutions

- **Bandwidth Cost vs. Video UX (Streaming Optimization):** Infinite scrolling video feeds consume immense resources. Relying on raw .mp4 files would drastically bloat S3 billing and cause UI stuttering.
  - **Solution:**Architected a streaming pipeline utilizing the HLS protocol (.m3u8) to serve videos in small, manageable chunks. I engineered a Smart Preloading strategy (buffering only the first 5-10s of the next 5 videos) coupled with the Intersection Observer API. The application actively monitors the viewport to pause and unload off-screen videos from memory. This slashed unnecessary bandwidth waste and infrastructure costs while ensuring a flawless, TikTok-like scrolling experience.

- **Complex Authorization & Team Velocity (RBAC & Leadership):** The platform required deeply separated workflows and workspaces for Employers vs. Candidates, while keeping a multi-FE team fully aligned.
  - **Solution:** Engineered a robust, client-side Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) architecture to cleanly handle permission logic. As a Lead, I instituted rigorous agile practices, including precise task estimations and a strict cross-code review culture. This eliminated knowledge silos and ensured high code quality and predictable delivery timelines across the team.

## Result

Successfully build a high-performance, highly cost-optimized video recruitment platform.
