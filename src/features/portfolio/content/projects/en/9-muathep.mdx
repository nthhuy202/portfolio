## Problem

The enterprise required a real-time B2B steel pricing portal to maximize pricing transparency for corporate buyers. The system needed robust data visualization (time-series charts) for historical price comparisons, seamlessly integrated with a "Quote Request" flow for lead generation. Furthermore, the platform demanded an aggressive SEO strategy, anchored by a market news Blog, to drive organic inbound traffic.

## Responsibilities

The engineering team architected the frontend infrastructure using Next.js, managed global states with the lightweight Zustand library, integrated Chart.js for commodity data visualization, and constructed a highly optimized Technical SEO foundation.

## Challenges & Solutions

- **High-Frequency Data State & Chart Performance:** Filtering and cross-comparing multiple steel variants over various timeframes involves processing dense time-series datasets. Poor state management would lead to UI freezing and sluggish chart re-rendering.
  - **Solution:** The team adopted Zustand over heavier alternatives (like Redux) for lean, decentralized state management. By isolating the chart's data streams from the main UI component tree and leveraging precise Memoization with Chart.js, the system enabled instant, lag-free cross-filtering and historical data comparison for end-users.

- **SEO for Highly Dynamic Data & Market Content:** Steel prices update frequently, yet they must be rapidly indexed by search engines. Meanwhile, the Blog module required lightning-fast load times to retain readership.
  - **Solution:** Maximized Next.js’s rendering capabilities. The team implemented ISR (Incremental Static Regeneration) for the pricing pages, ensuring data remained real-time while retaining the TTFB (Time to First Byte) speed of static pages. For the Blog section, SSG (Static Site Generation) combined with JSON-LD (Article Schema) was strictly applied, propelling market analysis articles to high search rankings and significantly boosting inbound B2B quote requests.

## Result

Successfully built a highly transparent, performant B2B commodity portal. The fluid charting experience empowered buyers to make data-driven decisions, while the robust SEO architecture transformed the platform into a powerful lead-generation engine for the business.
