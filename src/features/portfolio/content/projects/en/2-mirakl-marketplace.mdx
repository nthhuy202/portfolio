## Problem

The client needed a way to see business performance on the Mirakl Marketplace platform — orders, revenue, inventory — without digging through the platform's native admin views.

## Responsibilities

Built UI components from mockups, integrated APIs to manage the product lifecycle (variants, quantities), and rendered sales and inventory insights through dynamic ApexCharts visualizations.

## Result

Delivered as part of a 3-person team, giving the client a single dashboard for order, revenue and inventory data instead of piecing it together from the marketplace's own admin.
