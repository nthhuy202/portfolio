## Problem

The client wanted a fast corporate site plus a chat bot they could drop into other codebases as a package, rather than a one-off feature tied to this site alone.

## Responsibilities

Built a lightweight, high-performance landing page in pure HTML/CSS with AOS animations, plus accessibility attributes for SEO and assistive tech. Built the chat bot UI in Vue with Pinia for state, wiring real-time messaging over Socket.IO.

## Result

Shipped as an npm/yarn-installable component that introduces and summarizes site content — reusable in any codebase, not just the one it launched on.
